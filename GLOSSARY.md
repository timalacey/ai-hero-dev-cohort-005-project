# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to Learners. This glossary fixes the language for what happens when a Learner wants their money back.

## Language

### Learners and Purchases

**Learner**:
A person who buys and watches courses on Cadence.
_Avoid_: Customer, buyer, student, user

**Purchase**:
One Learner buying one course once, at the price they paid. A Learner who buys the same course twice has two Purchases, each with its own Refund Window.
_Avoid_: Order, transaction, enrollment

**Purchasing-Power Price**:
A course price adjusted down for the country the Learner buys from.
_Avoid_: PPP, regional discount

**Team Seat**:
One place on a team licence that a Learner can take to get access to a course.
_Avoid_: Coupon, code

**Claimed**:
The state of a Team Seat once a Learner has taken it; a Claimed Team Seat belongs to the team, not the Learner.
_Avoid_: Redeemed

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A Learner's ask to hand back a Purchase and get their money returned, together with their reason. It is either Requested, Approved or Declined.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, Reversal, Cancellation, cancel my purchase, Pending Chargeback, refund (for the ask itself)

**Refund**:
Money actually returned to a Learner, which exists only once an Admin approves a Refund Request. A Declined Refund Request produces no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback

**Requested**:
The state of a Refund Request that is waiting for an Admin to decide.
_Avoid_: Pending, open, raised

**Approved**:
The state of a Refund Request that an Admin has accepted, resulting in a Refund.

**Declined**:
The state of a Refund Request that an Admin has turned down; nothing is returned.
_Avoid_: Rejected, denied

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a Purchase, counted in whole days, during which a Refund Request may be raised.
_Avoid_: Cancel window

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a Purchase may have a Refund Request raised against it: it must be inside the Refund Window, the Learner must have watched less than two hours and completed less than half of the course's lessons, and it must not be a Claimed Team Seat.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer

**Refundable Amount**:
The amount a Refund would return, which is always what the Learner paid for the Purchase.
_Avoid_: Reversal amount, refund price

**Admin**:
A person on the Cadence team who approves or declines Refund Requests by hand.
_Avoid_: Reviewer

### Not modelled

**Chargeback**:
A dispute a cardholder raises through their bank to claw a payment back, with Cadence as the defending party. Cadence does not model Chargebacks; never use the word for a Refund Request.

**Reversal**:
Not a Cadence term. In payments it usually means voiding an authorisation before it settles; say Refund Request or Refund instead.
